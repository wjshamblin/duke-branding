# Other Communications and Media

Email signatures, social graphics, video, virtual backgrounds, digital signage,
event materials and merchandise. Colors, fonts and logo rules are the same as
everywhere else. This file covers what is specific to each format. Resolve group
values with `python3 scripts/duke_brand.py tokens --group <slug>`.

## Email signature

Duke publishes a standard Outlook signature and template:
https://communicators.duke.edu/uncategorized/outlook-email-signatures/. Follow its order:

```
Name (upper and lower case; Duke degrees and pronouns optional)
Title
Office/Dept/Center | Duke University | Box 90XXX, Durham, NC 277XX
email@duke.edu (all lower case)
(919) XXX-XXXX – office | (919) XXX-XXXX – mobile
```

- Plain text plus at most one logo image. System fonts only (Arial or Georgia),
  10 to 11pt, Cast Iron or Navy. Links in Prussian Blue.
- The line "Office/Dept/Center" comes from the group profile `name`.
- No quotes, no decorative backgrounds, no images of text. Must read fine with
  images blocked.

## Social media graphics

| Platform / use                 | Size (px)    |
|--------------------------------|--------------|
| Square post (Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook) | 1080 x 1080 |
| Portrait post (Instagram)      | 1080 x 1350  |
| Story / Reel / TikTok cover    | 1080 x 1920  |
| Landscape link or X/LinkedIn post | 1200 x 628 |
| Event cover                    | 1920 x 1005  |
| Avatar                         | 400 x 400, designed to survive a circular crop |

Platform specs drift, so confirm sizes for anything high-stakes.

- Build as SVG or HTML and export to PNG, so text stays sharp.
- Solid Navy field, or a photo with a solid Navy panel carrying the text. Headline
  in the heading font, 8 words or fewer. One accent.
- Keep text and logos inside the central 80 percent. On 1080 x 1920 leave the top
  250px and bottom 340px clear for platform UI.
- Avatar: the group `logo.symbol`. Never crop the Duke wordmark into an avatar.
- Text contrast rules apply to images too. Write alt text for every post.
- Duke's social video graphics package:
  https://communicators.duke.edu/social-media/a-guide-to-dukes-social-media-video-graphics-package/

## Video

Official guidance: https://communicators.duke.edu/multimedia/duke-video-strategy/

- 1920 x 1080 (or 3840 x 2160). Title-safe area: inner 90 percent.
- Title and end cards: solid Navy, white heading-font title, accent rule, reversed
  wordmark. The end card carries the group logo on the same baseline and the URL.
- Lower thirds: Navy bar, name in Open Sans Bold white, title in Open Sans Regular
  Whisper Gray, thin accent stripe on the leading edge.
- Captions are required. Open Sans, white on a Cast Iron panel at 75 percent opacity
  or more. (The no-transparency rule is about the Duke blues, not caption panels.)
- A wordmark bug, if used, sits in a corner at low visual weight and respects clear space.

## Virtual backgrounds (Zoom, Teams)

1920 x 1080. Keep the center empty, since that is where the speaker is. Wordmark
upper left, group logo upper right, both above shoulder height. Whisper Gray or a
quiet campus photo works better than solid Navy, which fights with skin tones and
dark clothing. Platforms mirror the self-view only; the audience sees it correctly.

## Digital signage and event materials

- Signage: 1920 x 1080 landscape or 1080 x 1920 portrait. Readable in 5 seconds:
  one headline, one detail line, one action. Body 40px or larger. High contrast only:
  white or Dandelion on Navy, Navy or Cast Iron on white.
- Name badges: name in Open Sans Bold 28pt or larger, affiliation in Regular, Navy
  band with the reversed wordmark.
- Banners and table drapes: vector logos, Pantone values, keep critical content clear
  of hems and grommets. Get a printer proof.

## Merchandise, apparel and anything sold or given away

Not a design-it-yourself category. Products bearing Duke marks go through Trademark
Licensing and licensed vendors:
https://communicators.duke.edu/design/trademark-licensing/. Tell the user this up
front, then help with a concept mock-up if they still want one, labelled as a
mock-up pending approval. Athletic marks and the Blue Devil are out of scope.
