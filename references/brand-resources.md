# Official Duke Brand Resources

Index of every official online source. Link to these in deliverable notes when the
user needs an asset this skill cannot bundle. Verified September 2026.

**Bundled already:** both wordmarks (SVG and PNG, four colors) and the four
sub-branding reference PDFs are in `assets/logos/`. Everything else below is a link.

**Access:** files under `assets.styleguide.duke.edu/internal/` and
`assets.brand.duke.edu/internal/` require Duke NetID login. They cannot be fetched
from a script or by an AI agent. Give the user the link and ask them to download.

Word, InDesign and similar; load by `<link>` for web.

## Units with their own brand centers

Duke Health and the School of Medicine keep separate standards and templates. For
work under those units, use theirs first.

- Duke Health brand center: https://corporate.dukehealth.org/duke-health-brand-center/powerpoint-letterhead-and-digital-assets
- School of Medicine templates: https://medschool.duke.edu/about-us/leadership-and-administration/office-strategic-communications/branding-identity-guide-1

Before building a group profile, search for "<unit> brand guidelines site:duke.edu".
Record what turns up in the profile's `brand_guide_url`.
